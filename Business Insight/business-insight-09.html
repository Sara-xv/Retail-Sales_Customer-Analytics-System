Business Insight 09 — Monthly Sales and Gross Margin Stability

Key Finding

Monthly gross margin remains relatively stable throughout the analysis period, despite variations in monthly sales.

Supporting Evidence

  • Monthly gross margin remains within a narrow range of approximately 31.95% to 32.27%.
  • Monthly Net Sales generally fall within a relatively consistent range, with lower totals in shorter months.
  • The observed variation in monthly sales does not correspond to a major change in overall gross margin.

Business Implication

The dataset suggests that fluctuations in sales value are not accompanied by substantial changes in aggregate gross margin. Further analysis is required to distinguish calendar effects, changes in sales volume, and product-level effects.
