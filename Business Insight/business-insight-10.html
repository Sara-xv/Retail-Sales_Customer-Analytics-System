Business Insight 10 — Annual Sales and Profitability Trend

Key Finding

Net Sales and Gross Profit show a gradual decline from 2023 through 2025, while gross margin remains stable.

Supporting Evidence

  • Net Sales declined from approximately 564.19 billion in 2023 to 559.75 billion in 2025.
  • Gross Profit Before Returns declined from approximately 181.12 billion in 2023 to 179.65 billion in 2025.
  • Gross margin remained close to 32.10% throughout the three-year period.
  • Year-over-year Net Sales growth was -0.13% in 2024 and -0.66% in 2025.

Business Implication

The overall decline appears to be more closely associated with lower sales activity and slightly lower average sales value than with a substantial deterioration in gross margin. Further investigation is needed to identify the underlying drivers.
