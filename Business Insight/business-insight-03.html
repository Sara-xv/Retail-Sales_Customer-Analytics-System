Business Insight 03 — Discount and Gross Margin by Sales Channel

Finding

The online channel has a higher discount rate than the physical-store channel (12.14% versus 11.49%), while its gross margin is slightly lower (31.71% versus 32.21%). This pattern suggests that discounting may be one factor associated with the margin difference, but the current analysis does not establish causation.

Recommended Next Step

Further analysis should compare product mix, discount levels, and profitability within comparable product categories to determine whether the observed margin gap persists after accounting for differences in sales composition.

Data Limitation

The dataset is synthetic; these findings describe the dataset and should not be interpreted as evidence of actual retail-market behavior.
