Business Insight 15 — High-Selling Footwear–Sandals Products and Margin Performance

Key Finding

High-selling products in the Footwear–Sandals subcategory generate substantial revenue but have gross margins below the simple average across all products.

Supporting Evidence

  • The selected products have gross margins ranging from approximately 24.82% to 25.25%.
  • Their margins are approximately 7.67 to 8.10 percentage points below the simple average product margin of 32.92%.
  • The highest-selling product in the selected group, PRD00538, generates approximately 4.83 billion in Net Sales and 1.21 billion in Gross Profit, with a gross margin of 24.97%.

Business Implication

These products warrant further investigation into unit costs, discounting, and pricing. Their substantial sales contribution means that even a modest improvement in margin could be financially meaningful. However, margin optimization should be evaluated alongside sales volume and customer demand.

Note

The benchmark is the unweighted average of product-level gross margins, not the company's aggregate gross margin. All figures are based on synthetic project data, and profitability is measured before returns.
