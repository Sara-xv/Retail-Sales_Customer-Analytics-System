Business Insight 01 — Category Revenue and Profitability

Finding

Clothing is the primary revenue and gross profit contributor, generating 64.70% of net sales before returns and 69.32% of gross profit before returns. Footwear contributes 31.44% of revenue but only 25.87% of gross profit. Accessories contributes a relatively small share of revenue (3.86%) while maintaining a gross margin of 40.00%.

Business Interpretation

Clothing is the main contributor to the company's gross profit. Footwear's lower gross margin warrants further investigation into product costs, discounting, and product mix. Accessories may offer a potential growth opportunity, but its small revenue contribution means that demand and operational feasibility should be evaluated before making investment decisions.

Recommended Next Step

Analyze category-level discount rates and gross margins to investigate whether discounting contributes to the profitability gap between Clothing and Footwear.

Data Limitation

The dataset is synthetic. These findings describe the generated dataset and should not be interpreted as evidence of real-world retail market behavior.

Financial Scope

All revenue and profit figures are calculated before the effect of returns.
