Business Insight 02 — Discount Impact on Category Profitability

Finding

Footwear has the highest discount rate (18.46%) and the largest total discount impact on gross profit, approximately 120.10 billion monetary units. Clothing has a lower discount rate (8.54%) and a discount impact of approximately 101.96 billion monetary units. Accessories has no recorded discounts.

Interpretation

In the synthetic dataset, all three categories have a 40.00% gross margin before discounts. The differences in gross margin after discounts are therefore consistent with the different discount patterns across categories.

Business Implication

Footwear discounting is a priority for further analysis. The business should evaluate whether these discounts generate enough incremental sales to justify their impact on gross profit.

Limitation

This is a synthetic dataset and a static financial comparison. It does not establish the causal effect of discounts on customer demand or sales volume.
