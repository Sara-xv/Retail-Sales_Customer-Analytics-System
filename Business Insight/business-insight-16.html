Business Insight 16 — Low-Margin Products Across Clothing and Footwear

Key Finding

The bottom 20 products by gross margin have margins ranging from 24.63% to 24.82%. These products span both Clothing and Footwear, suggesting that low-margin performance is not exclusive to a single product category.

Supporting Evidence

However, lower gross margin does not necessarily mean lower gross profit. For example, PRD00118 (Sandals) generated approximately 4.81 billion in net sales and 1.19 billion in gross profit at a 24.66% margin, while PRD00383 (Jackets) generated approximately 397.13 million in net sales and 97.81 million in gross profit at a similar margin.

Business Implication

Prioritize product-level margin analysis alongside sales volume and gross profit. Compare products against their own category and subcategory benchmarks before deciding whether pricing, discounting, or cost optimization is needed.

Note

Figures are from a synthetic dataset and represent gross profit before returns.
